## MODULE 1: B2B SALES & CLIENT ACQUISITION (100 PROMPTS)

### 1. High-Ticket Cold Email Generator
```text
Act as a world-class B2B copywriter who generates $100k+ enterprise deals. Write a 4-sentence cold email to [Target Role, e.g., Chief Marketing Officer] at [Company Type, e.g., Luxury Real Estate in Dubai]. 
Focus on solving their biggest headache: [Pain Point, e.g., acquiring high-net-worth foreign property investors at lower CAC]. 
Rules:
- No generic buzzwords ("synergy", "paradigm shift").
- Mention a specific insight about their market.
- Soft CTA proposing a 3-minute Loom video or a 5-minute WhatsApp call.
- Sign off as Ismail Kazia, Founder of SAMRAT WORLDWIDE.
```

### 2. WhatsApp Direct Outreach Script
```text
Create 3 variations of high-converting WhatsApp outreach messages for [Target Business, e.g., Bangalore Tech Startups planning a Hackathon/Summit].
- Variation 1: Short & Direct (Under 50 words).
- Variation 2: Value-First (Offers a free venue comparison or budget blueprint).
- Variation 3: Social Proof / Case Study (Mentions 400+ attendee summit executed flawlessly).
Include natural emojis and a frictionless reply trigger.
```

### 3. Objection Handling Playbook
```text
Generate a rebuttal script for the top 7 B2B objections:
1. "Your price is too high."
2. "We already have an existing vendor."
3. "Send me a proposal by email (Brush-off)."
4. "We don't have budget this quarter."
5. "I need to discuss with my board/partner."
6. "We do this in-house."
7. "Contact us next year."
Format each with: The Psychology Behind the Objection -> The Exact Words to Say -> The Follow-Up Question to Regain Control.
```

### 4. Client Proposal Auto-Generator
```text
You are a senior enterprise strategist. Generate a structured, premium B2B proposal for [Client Name] for our service: [Service Name, e.g., End-to-End Corporate Tech Summit Production in Bangalore].
Include:
1. Executive Summary & Problem Diagnosis
2. Proposed Solution Architecture (Stage, 4K AV, VIP Hospitality)
3. 3-Tier Investment Options (Standard, Royal/Elite, Enterprise)
4. Production Timeline & Milestone Roadmap (Weeks 1 to 4)
5. Risk Reversal & SLA Guarantee
Tone: Authoritative, polished, ultra-exclusive.
```
