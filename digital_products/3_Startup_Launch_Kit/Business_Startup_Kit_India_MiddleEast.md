## 🏛️ PART 1: BUSINESS REGISTRATION & COMPLIANCE ROADMAP

### A. INDIA (Sole Proprietorship, LLP, Private Limited)
1. **PAN & Aadhaar Linking**: Mandatory baseline for founder KYC.
2. **MSME / Udyam Registration**: 100% Free, online government portal (provides loan subsidy & priority payment protection under MSME Act).
3. **GST Registration**:
   - Mandatory if turnover exceeds ₹20 Lakhs (services) or ₹40 Lakhs (goods), or for any interstate/export billing.
   - Requires: Electricity bill/rent agreement, NOC from owner, Bank cancelled cheque.
4. **Current Account Setup**:
   - Preferred: HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, or Kotak Mahindra.
   - Zero-balance digital options: RazorpayX / Jupiter Business.
5. **Trademark (Class 35 / Class 41)**: Protect your brand name before launching publicly.

### B. SAUDI ARABIA (Individual Freelancer vs CR)
1. **Freelance Work Document (وثيقة العمل الحر)**:
   - **Cost:** FREE.
   - **Requirement:** Saudi National ID or Iqama with eligible profession.
   - **Benefit:** Allows opening a legal bank account in Saudi Arabia, integrating local payment gateways (Mada, Apple Pay, STC Pay) without registering a formal commercial company (No CR needed!).
2. **Commercial Registration (CR / السجل التجاري)**:
   - Through Ministry of Commerce (موقع وزارة التجارة).
   - Ministry of Investment (MISA) for foreign investor licenses.
3. **ZATCA (Fatoora) E-Invoicing**: Mandatory QR-code compliant electronic billing.
